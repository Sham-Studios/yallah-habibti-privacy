Support — Yallah Habibti

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Questions, problems, or feedback about Yallah Habibti — the fastest way to reach us is sfnfretail@gmail.com. A few common questions are answered below first.

Restoring a Premium purchase

Open the app → Settings → Restore purchases. This restores an active subscription tied to the Apple ID or Google account you originally subscribed with — no account with us is required.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely through Apple or Google, not inside the app:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings app → [your name] → Subscriptions → Yallah Habibti.
  • Android: Play Store app → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Yallah Habibti.

Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Premium access until the end of the period you already paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own refund policies.

Syncing progress across devices

From Settings → Sync my progress across devices, sign in with your Google or Apple account. This is entirely optional — the app works fully without it.

Deleting your account and cloud data

From Settings → Delete account and cloud data. This permanently removes your account, your synced progress, and any leaderboard entries from our systems. It does not touch the progress already saved on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Reporting a leaderboard name

Tap the flag icon next to any name on a leaderboard — this opens a pre-filled email to us, and we'll act on genuine reports.

Something not working, or a bug to report

Email sfnfretail@gmail.com with what happened, your device model, and iOS/Android version if you can. We read every message.
